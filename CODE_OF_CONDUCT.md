# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We are committed to a harassment-free, welcoming, and respectful community for everyone,
regardless of background or identity. In short:

- Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith.
- No harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or hateful conduct.
- Critique ideas and code, not people. Keep discussion on-topic and evidence-based
  (fitting for a project about sourced, governed claims).

Unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the maintainers via GitHub (open a private
security advisory or contact a maintainer). Maintainers may remove content or contributors who
violate these norms.
